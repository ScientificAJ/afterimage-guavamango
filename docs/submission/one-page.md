# AFTERIMAGE

## One sentence

AFTERIMAGE is an autonomous Alpaca options agent that must survive an outcome-blind evidence gate and receive a cryptographic, single-use execution warrant before it can place an exact paper Mleg order.

## Why it exists

Trading agents can generate polished explanations after a result is known. They can also silently discard failed variants. AFTERIMAGE makes both shortcuts difficult to hide. It freezes the market claim, selects historical twins without outcomes, commits the entire cohort, reveals outcomes only afterward, records every failed strategy, and lets deterministic policy—not the language model—decide whether capital authority exists.

## The six-step proof

1. **Market Claim:** a versioned strategy freezes a point-in-time snapshot and emits a falsifiable candidate.
2. **Sealed Jury:** in live mode, twins come from a hash-validated Alpaca training partition and are selected from features while dates, identities, paths, returns, and labels remain inaccessible. Recorded controls use an explicitly synthetic fixture.
3. **Reveal:** the committed cohort unlocks and deterministic code calculates effective sample size, Wilson bound, cost-aware expectancy, drawdown, stability, and placebo separation.
4. **3R Passport:** executable leg prices, slippage, fees, operational target, operational stop, position loss, daily loss, drawdown, and account state pass an ordered risk vector.
5. **Execution Warrant:** Ed25519 signs the exact paper account hash, strategy, evidence, legs, ratios, quantity, maximum debit, risk vector, nonce, and expiration.
6. **Broker Truth:** official Alpaca CLI submission is idempotent; after ambiguity or restart, Alpaca orders and positions override local memory. Filled positions are watchdog-managed through separately signed target, stop, or time exits and a broker-receipt autopsy.

## What is original

- Verifiable non-cherry-picking through pre-registration, blind retrieval, commit/reveal, and an Alpha Graveyard.
- Information-separated agents whose access differs by role, not just by prompt wording.
- Evidence-bound execution: editing any authorized order field breaks the warrant.
- `NO_CHAMPION` and `NO_TRADE` are valid successful outcomes.

## What is demonstrated

The accepted and rejected controls are deterministic synthetic engineering
demonstrations, labeled at the point of display. A separate live-shadow run has
authenticated the dedicated Alpaca paper account, verified Level 3, zero exposure,
and used real market bars. The real research artifact contains 32,346 SPY IEX bars;
the strategy-derived chronological tournament promotes no champion. Historical
option validation matched all 12 opening-range signals and reports -$126 for a
one-lot sequence after modeled slippage. That is real trade-bar evidence, but not
historical bid/ask or broker P&L. The exact `zai-org/GLM-5.3` and
`zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash` routes are never substituted. Automated tests cover the
candidate-present path through Alpaca dry-run and the complete fake-broker lifecycle
through fill, signed risk-reducing exit, and broker-receipt autopsy. L5 remains empty
until a naturally qualifying paper setup fills and exits.
