# AFTERIMAGE — The trade is the last step of the proof

Most trading agents explain a decision. AFTERIMAGE proves the decision process was not quietly rewritten after seeing the answer.

When a strategy finds a setup, AFTERIMAGE freezes the exact point-in-time market snapshot. A sealed historical twin trial then retrieves comparable situations without exposing their dates or outcomes. The cohort, weights, exclusions, strategy version, dataset, prompts, and model versions are durably committed. Only then are outcomes revealed. Deterministic code—not an LLM—checks effective sample size, a declared confidence bound, cost-aware expectancy, drawdown, chronological stability, leave-one-cluster-out stability, placebo edge, and data coverage.

Passing evidence is still not permission to trade. The proposed Alpaca options structure must clear structural and conservative operational reward/risk of at least 3:1, use executable bid/ask prices, fit a $500 modeled-loss ceiling, and satisfy account, clock, liquidity, exposure, daily-loss, and drawdown gates. Only then does a local Ed25519 authority issue a short-lived, single-use warrant bound to the exact Level 3 Mleg intent. One changed price, leg, quantity, account, strategy, evidence hash, or deadline makes the warrant invalid.

## P&L Performance

AFTERIMAGE separates underlying replay, historical option-quote replay, shadow decisions, and Alpaca paper fills into explicit evidence levels. It reports distributions, cost-aware R expectancy, drawdown, confidence bounds, censoring, and modeled-versus-broker slippage instead of promoting one screenshot. No 3:1 payoff is claimed unless both the structural payoff and the operational target/stop payoff clear 3R after modeled costs.

The implementation currently includes deterministic accepted and rejected recorded-paper fixtures. These are orchestration evidence, not claimed live returns. Controlled forward paper outcomes and real replay metrics belong to the validation pass and will remain distinguishable in the interface.

## Technology Implementation

- Python typed contracts, generic strategy plugins, a deterministic state machine, SQLite WAL persistence, and a hash-chained audit ledger.
- Separate least-privilege official Alpaca MCP processes for market data and paper trading, with capability-schema hashes and a paper-account preflight.
- Provider-neutral structured-output agents backed by Featherless, with strict schemas, redaction, budgets, timeouts, abstention, and a recorded fallback.
- Outcome and feature vault separation, content-addressed evidence, and commit/reveal verification.
- Deterministic payoff, sizing, risk, Ed25519 warrant, idempotent client-order ID, reconciliation, and watchdog modules.
- FastAPI, resumable SSE, CLI operations, evidence export, and a responsive Next.js judge cockpit.

## Creativity & Originality

The novelty is not another multi-agent debate. It is a chain of custody for trading authority:

**pre-register → retrieve blind → commit → reveal → challenge → verify risk → sign exact intent → reconcile broker truth.**

The sealed strategy tournament evaluates all frozen candidates on the same holdout. Every loser remains visible in the Alpha Graveyard. A ranking score cannot override a failed hard gate, and the tournament may promote no champion.

## Presentation & Execution

The cockpit tells the entire story in six visible moments: Market Claim, Sealed Jury, Ghost Paths, 3R Passport, Execution Warrant, and Broker Truth. Every headline fact carries an evidence level or artifact hash. A second one-click replay demonstrates that insufficient evidence terminates safely without a warrant or broker call.

## Safety and scope

AFTERIMAGE is paper-only, forces an allowlisted account with effective Options Level 3, and disables paper order submission by default. It does not provide investment advice or guarantee returns. Real credentials are excluded from Git, exported artifacts, screenshots, and prompts.
