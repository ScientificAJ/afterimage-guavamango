“Now—and only now—outcomes unlock. Deterministic code computes effective sample size, the pre-declared Wilson lower bound, cost-aware expectancy, drawdown, cluster stability, and placebos. No model decides whether the math passed.”

Open **3R Passport**, then **Execution Warrant**.

“The spread clears both structural and conservative operational 3R after cost, while the maximum modeled position loss stays below $500. The Ed25519 warrant signs the exact account, evidence, legs, ratios, quantity, price, and TTL. Edit one field and it fails.”

## 1:05–1:18 — Broker truth

Point to **Broker Truth**.

“Submission is idempotent, and official Alpaca CLI state—not the UI or model—is
authoritative after a timeout or restart. This control reaches a local order dry-run;
it does not claim a broker fill or live profit. In execution mode, a fill enters an
exact-position watchdog; target, stop, and time exits require a second signed closing
warrant, and only broker receipts may populate the L5 autopsy.”

## 1:18–1:30 — The rejection proof

Click **Rejection proof**.

“A winning agent must know when not to trade. Here the same pipeline fails its sealed evidence gate. No risk approval, no warrant, no broker call. Every rejected strategy remains below in the Alpha Graveyard, so selection bias cannot disappear from the story.”

Close with: “AFTERIMAGE does not ask you to trust its reasoning. It gives you the receipts.”

If connectivity is stable, finish by selecting **Live shadow**. Show the official
CLI transport, zero open orders/positions, real bar and contract counts, the exact
