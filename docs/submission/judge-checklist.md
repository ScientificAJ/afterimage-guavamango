# Judge-proof checklist

## P&L Performance

- [ ] Separate L1 underlying, L2 option-quote, L3 sealed-trial, L4 shadow, and L5 Alpaca paper evidence.
- [ ] Show timestamped option quotes, executable bid/ask assumptions, fees, and slippage.
- [ ] Show expectancy distribution, confidence bound, drawdown, coverage, censoring, and sample size.
- [ ] Compare conservative modeled P&L with broker paper P&L; never call a fixture live P&L.
- [ ] Freeze the final holdout and forward-paper cutoff before evaluation.

## Technology Implementation

- [ ] Demonstrate official Alpaca MCP tool discovery and sanitized schema hashes.
- [ ] Show paper-account allowlist, ACTIVE state, Level 3, zero unknown exposure, and paper endpoint.
- [ ] Demonstrate accepted replay reaching AUTOPSY and rejection replay ending before warrant.
- [ ] Demonstrate tamper, expiry, replay, and idempotency controls during the verification pass.
- [ ] Export and independently recompute audit/evidence hashes.

## Creativity & Originality

- [ ] Make blind retrieval and commit-before-reveal visible within 30 seconds.
- [ ] Show one rejected candidate in the Alpha Graveyard.
- [ ] Explain why role-separated information is more than a council of personas.
- [ ] Show that `NO_CHAMPION` and `NO_TRADE` are valid results.
- [ ] Demonstrate that changing one warrant-bound field removes authority.

## Presentation & Execution

- [ ] Keep the primary story within 90 seconds and avoid raw transcripts.
- [ ] Record the deterministic fallback before touching the final demo environment.
- [ ] Confirm cockpit at 1440×900, mobile, keyboard navigation, and reduced motion.
- [ ] Verify every claim links to a source artifact or exact reason code.
- [ ] Keep credentials, account IDs, and recovery material out of video and evidence exports.
