# AFTERIMAGE film design system

## Creative thesis

The film behaves like an evidence dossier learning to move: clean white paper,
hard black telemetry, mango signal energy, cobalt proof, and coral challenge.
Motion is rapid but exact. Nothing glows, floats, or decorates without carrying
meaning. The Flight Deck remains the visual source of truth.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#FFFFFF`
- Paper mist: `#F2F2EF`
- Ink: `#11110F`
- Mango / signal: `#F3AD00`
- Cobalt / proof: `#203EE4`
- Coral / critic: `#FF655F`
- Green / broker pass: `#13883C`
- Hairline: `#D7D6D1`

## Typography

- Display: Barlow Condensed, 600–700, uppercase, tight tracking.
- Body: DM Sans Variable, 400–560.
- Data: JetBrains Mono Variable, 500–700, tabular figures.

## Composition

1920×1080. White is the permanent ground. Use asymmetric 12-column grids,
strict rectangles, 1px rules, and oversized editorial type. Product captures
sit inside crisp crop windows without device mockups. Captions occupy the lower
180px safe band. Research metrics always carry a `RESEARCH EVIDENCE` label;
broker state always carries `ALPACA PAPER`.

## Motion

Motion follows the data path from left to right. Type arrives through short
masked rises; hairlines draw; signals strike in mango; evidence locks in cobalt;
the critic interrupts in coral. UI shots use measured pan-and-scan moves with no
elastic easing. Transitions are match cuts through rules, grids, or shared words.

## Audio

User-recorded narration is the authority layer. “Algorithmic Flow” is the
music bed, trimmed to the film, high-passed around the narration, ducked roughly
10–14 dB while speech is active, and allowed to breathe only at the hook, metric
reveal, workflow cadence, and closing logo hit.
